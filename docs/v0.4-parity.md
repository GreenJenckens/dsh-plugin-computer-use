## 7. Verdict

**Capability parity is credible on observation, actions, DX, cost structure and distribution. Safety is the one material gap: we ship a deterministic pre-authorization gate and an on-desktop Разрешить/Запретить dialog, but not a transparent human takeover, and the host runs outside the DSH sandbox.** The docs therefore state: *engine parity with documented safety differences*, not “we are better than Anthropic/OpenAI”.

Closed through v0.5: batched `act.sequence`, subagent default-deny, auto/readonly/disabled modes, `observe.wait`, `diagnostics`, `expectedWindow` stale-capture fence, terminal/password/keyword confirmation dialog, cancel hotkey and activity indicator, per-monitor DPI reporting, Chromium password regression, OCR oversize clamp, retention/prune tooling, reproducible build/verify.

Still open (v0.5 candidates): transparent human takeover/handoff, stable element refs, normalized coordinates, full 20-task eval on a disposable VM, hardware validation for 2 monitors/125–150%, signed distribution.

## 8. Where we deliberately do not compete

- Model-side visual grounding and reasoning quality (the LLM, not the harness).
- Hosted sandbox/VM orchestration and CAPTCHA/2FA as product features.
- Token pricing and OSWorld/WebArena benchmark parity — we cannot run the vendors’ models here.
- macOS/Linux backends and MCP packaging in v0.4.
